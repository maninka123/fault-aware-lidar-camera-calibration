# Paper-to-algorithm decisions

Research cut-off: **5 November 2026**. This is a design gate and a provisional originality audit, not a claim of a new published method. Links identify primary sources. A missing entry below is not evidence that a technique has never been published.

## Access and interpretation

The arXiv HTML methods were inspected for A1–A3, A5–A6, B2, C1–C2 and D1. A4 was available as an institutional abstract; its exact objective and experimental details remain unverified. B1's author supplement and released inference implementation were inspected; the journal full text was not retrieved at this gate. C3's accepted-manuscript PDF was located at Oxford; extraction status is recorded in the prior-art review. Interpretations below distinguish observed method content from our proposed translation. Numbers reported by other authors are not our benchmark results.

## Candidate mechanism: correspondence-stable conditional fault contrasts

The following is the **specific proposed adaptation to implement and falsify**, subject to the mathematical method and tests. It is not an established novelty claim.

For each training-only association variant `v`, form a dimensionlessly scaled measurement Jacobian `A_v = W_v^(1/2) J_v S`. Split columns into rotation, translation, camera parameters and explicitly modeled nuisance. To assess a group `g` after allowing rivals and nuisance `B_v`, compute

`C_(g,v) = (I - B_v B_v^+) A_(g,v)`.

This is established conditional-information/fault-isolation geometry. Obtain its supported right-space projector `P_(g,v)` by rank-revealing SVD without priors. Retain directions common to all variants: start from eigenvectors near one of `mean_v P_(g,v)` and verify the **worst-variant** norm/singular value on the retained basis. Approximate projector consensus alone is insufficient. Different variants may have different residual counts; compare projectors in the same parameter space, not residual vectors with incompatible rows. Empty common support means abstention, not proof of no drift.

Candidate generation can update a selected hypothesis's **joint observable** subspace. Attribution additionally requires persistent group-unique contrast; a joint identifiable fit need not identify a unique fault cause. Distinguish nested hypotheses using incremental dimensions: a rotation fit is contained in a rotation+translation model, so their shared effects cannot be made disjoint by a pairwise angle score. Reports preserve the observationally equivalent explanations rather than forcing a label.

Capture selection targets the weakest unresolved **contrast after profiling** over plausible association variants. It must use fit data and known capture/session blocks only. Independent held-out geometric loss validates candidates on the same admissible evaluation points; missing/invalid projections cannot disappear from one candidate's score. Complexity and uncertainty rules remain empirical unless explicitly proved. Never call nearest-edge loss true correspondence reprojection error.

### Implementation gate

Proceed as a research prototype only if the implementation:

1. Separates measurement rank from priors, group attribution from joint estimability, and fixed-association derivatives from association changes.
2. Verifies the contrast basis across all declared variants and reports the variant construction and sensitivity.
3. Treats unknown nuisances and too few independent sessions as evidence limitations.
4. Includes single-variant TSVD, conditional-only, no-selection and joint-fit comparators.
5. States that robust subspaces, maximin design and abstention are established; the proposed contribution is a tested camera–LiDAR decision procedure across correspondence alternatives.

**Originality gate:** still open. A* readiness cannot be concluded from the current review or a single recorded rig. A new theorem, calibrated risk statement, or reproducible evidence that the specific association-stability procedure changes decisions advantageously is needed to support a stronger contribution claim. A duplicate formulation found in robust FDI/experimental design would reduce this to an engineering adaptation.
