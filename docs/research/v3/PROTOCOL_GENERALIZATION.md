# Protocol: grouped generalization audit (frozen before the groups were run)

Written 6 November 2026. The constants, source files and group assignments are frozen by hash in `FROZEN_GENERALIZATION.json`
(`tools/run_generalization_v3.py --freeze`); the runner refuses to start if any differs, and a unit test fails if a frozen constant or source
file is edited later. Editing after seeing results is a new development round, not a test.

## Unit of independence

A **group** is one device in one session and state (one folder of captures). Captures within a group are one static scene; perturbations of a
recording are perturbations of the same scene. Neither is treated as independent. Groups are further clustered into **scenes** by measured image
similarity (ORB homography inliers between group median images, at least 20), because several devices photographed the same room. Folds are
leave-one-scene-out, leave-one-session-out and leave-one-device-out; a fold's training groups share no device, session or scene (as per the fold's key)
and no capture with the held-out groups (`generalization.assert_no_leakage`).

What each held-out role can and cannot show:

* **Cross-device, same scene and session** (pair_2, pair_3): different physical units of the same camera and LiDAR model viewing one scene. Shows unit-to-unit variation only.
* **Cross-session, same device** (Nov 5 against Oct 28, if it is the same unit, which is likely but not proven): later session, different capture conditions.
* **Cross-scene** (Nov 5): the only groups with a different scene and a deeper depth distribution. Two groups from one day: not two independent scenes.
* **Cross-sensor model**: **not available.** Every group uses the same fisheye camera model (484x366) and the same LiDAR model with its non-repetitive scanning pattern. A different LiDAR or scan pattern has not been tested.
* Not available at all: other lighting conditions chosen on purpose, reflective-surface scenes chosen on purpose, partial occlusions chosen on purpose, nuisance-only physical sessions. Image descriptors in the audit (brightness, saturation, edge density, range, angular spread) show how the groups differ, nothing more.

The Nov 5 and Oct 28 pair_2 / pair_3 recordings were seen by the desktop-application checks, and one Nov 5 capture overlay was inspected visually while fixing an application figure. No V2 or V3 experiment, design choice or
constant used them. They are **not blind test data**: the same lab, the same rig family and the same engineers; they are the best available held-out groups.

## Methods compared (same groups, same seeds, same fitting budgets)

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| Original method | The frozen maintenance optimizer (`legacy_optimizer` strategy) inside the common protocol; its decision is the engine's production policy applied to its fits. The full maintenance application is not re-run (minutes per trial). |
| Production policy | The research engine's fault-aware production policy. |
| V2, V2 without intensity | Fault Diagnostics V2 and the V2 decision without its harmful intensity rule. |
| Original V3 | The recorded pipeline of `fault_diagnostics_v3.runner` (geometric veto). |
| Improved V3 | Signed-residual diagnosis on responsibility-weighted pairs (`geometric`), with variants (`oriented`, no visibility, no balancing, no confidence). |

The first four share one set of production fits per trial. Improved V3 uses no optimizer fits. Seeds, capture subsets, perturbation directions and conditions are those of the V2/V3 tools.

## Conditions and truth

Per group, one replicate: nominal, five injected changes (rotation 1 degree, translation 6 cm, principal point 3 px, focal length 2 %, rotation plus translation) and, for the improved variants, two image nuisances (spurious lines, ghost edges).
Faster than earlier studies (seven drift conditions, two replicates) at the user's request; the omitted conditions are the smaller magnitudes.

Truth is relative to each group's **anchor**: the group's declared reference refined by the production engine on that group's own captures. The anchor defines the injected condition and the scoring; no method receives it; it is never reused across groups (the registry check
rejects one reference calibration shared by different devices). The deployment reference of a group is its own device's board calibration; for the Nov 5 groups it is the Oct 28 device-1 calibration, the file their own application checks used.
An anchor is evaluation truth, not physical truth.

## Leakage guards

No information from a held-out group is used for a constant, a threshold, a normalization, a candidate or variant selection, an anchor of another group or a model choice. Constants were fixed on the development groups before these groups were run
(frozen by hash). The leave-one-out selection of the pairing variant sees training groups only (`select_variant`, tested with a guard object that raises if a held-out group is read), with a cost declared here: (no-change alarms + wrong unique labels, then fewer
missed detections, then fewer misses of exact attribution) summed over training groups. Tests also check capture overlap, shared reference calibrations across devices, anchors built from foreign captures, that methods receive no truth fields,
and that the frozen constants and sources are unchanged.

## Reporting and predeclared expectations

Reported separately for each group (and device, session, scene): no-change alarms, detections, exact attribution, wrong unique attribution, ambiguity sets that contain the truth, recovery error (improved variants), seconds per trial; worst group per metric; and the gap between the mean over development groups and the mean over not-used groups.

1. The improved-V3 false-alarm rate on every not-used group is no higher than that of the original V3 on that group. A group where it is higher is the worst case and is reported.
2. Exact attribution of the improved V3 on the not-used groups is no higher than on the development groups (a non-negative generalization gap is expected, not an improvement).
3. Leave-one-out selection of the pairing variant does not beat the fixed default by a margin that could only come from the held-out group.
4. Any aggregate that hides a group with a worse result is flagged by the per-group tables.

## Out of scope and still unestablished

Generalization to other LiDAR models and scan patterns, other camera families, other scenes chosen for their properties, other sessions of a different rig, physical faults, statistical independence, calibrated error rates.
If the results below are good, they show consistency across these particular groups, not generalization.
